MUSIC

Put songs in this folder and DejaVuAmp lists them. Folders work too (music/Artist/Album/song.mp3, up to four levels down): the list shows them as a tree you open and close with a click. It plays MP3, FLAC, WAV and Ogg Vorbis (.mp3 .flac .wav .ogg .oga) and, when the program was built with ffmpeg, M4A/AAC, Opus and WMA (.m4a .aac .opus .wma).
Take a song out of the folder to remove it from the list. New and removed files are noticed within a couple of seconds; no restart needed.
You can also start the program with songs or folders:   dejavuamp song.mp3 "My Albums/"
